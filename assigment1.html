About TuteDude

TuteDude is a revolutionary online learning platform designed to make education accessible, engaging, and effective for students worldwide. We combine cutting-edge technology with expert instruction to deliver an unparalleled learning experience.

Our platform offers courses in STEM, humanities, and professional development. With over 1 million active learners and 500+ expert instructors, we're transforming education one student at a time.

TuteDude Trivia

Discover fascinating facts about our platform and the world of online learning!

Did you know about our founding story?

TuteDude was founded in 2018 by three college friends who struggled to find quality, affordable education online. Today, we've grown to serve students in over 150 countries!

What makes our teaching methodology unique?

We use a proprietary adaptive learning algorithm that adjusts course difficulty based on individual student performance, ensuring optimal learning pace for everyone.

How many courses have been completed?

Over 5 million courses have been successfully completed on our platform, with an average satisfaction rating of 4.8 out of 5 stars!

What's our student success rate?

An impressive 87% of our students report achieving their learning goals within their target timeframe, with many exceeding their own expectations!
